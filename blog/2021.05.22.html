A New Beginning


It has been more than six years since I stopped blogging. Life got busy with two kids and successfully growing a business.

Now we are in the middle of probably one of the most severe pandemics in our lifetime. It has been challenging for individuals to cope with the adjustments, such as social distancing, and rolling lock-downs. Outings with friends have become non-existent over the past two years. It is as if someone pulled the plug from social life and time started to race by. The last big family event feels like a very distant memory. I think only hindsight will show how many acquired mental and social disabilities we actually acquired as a nation.

With outside life being shut off, we worked on some home projects, made the best of working from home, and somewhat procrastinated on being good parents. Being bound at home makes you reflect if you are doing the right thing. I used to write about my projects. I slid slide into being a moderator for a few dedicated Facebook groups that fell into disrepair over the past few years. Its time to reopen and climb out of mental lock-down.

It is time to reclaim that inner nerdy child. If you are into retro or embedded computing, parenting, fitness I hope you will find something useful on this blog. Let me take you on a ride!
